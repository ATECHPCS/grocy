# Product research and approval for purchase capture

## Outcome and confirmed decisions

A household user scans purchases rapidly on a phone. If a valid scanned UPC/GTIN has no Grocy owner, Grocy queues research immediately and the companion consults Barcode Buddy Federation and Open Food Facts. The user later sees a product draft inside purchase capture review, corrects it if needed, and approves creation or links the scan to an existing product. The approved product and its barcode then become available to the existing receipt allocation review. Only **Commit purchase** adds stock.

The user explicitly chose **approve draft, then create**. Research and draft creation are automatic; normal Grocy product persistence is not. If neither provider identifies the code, create a clearly marked **provisional draft** from the code and, when reliably paired, the receipt description. Approval creates the Grocy product. An unmatched receipt-only item has no UPC job and retains the existing Include/Ignore review flow. Ignoring or deselecting a purchase never creates a product.

The first rollout must offer research for the 15 currently unknown lines in open trip #12 without changing its 17 scans, receipt #3, receipt decisions, or stock. The existing receipt completion, price, quantity, store, difference, checksum, and explicit commit rules remain authoritative.

## Chosen approach

Use a durable, bounded queue in the `grocy_AI` module and a companion worker. This keeps the scan request independent of provider latency and lets work resume after a Grocy or companion restart. A synchronous lookup during every scan would slow or interrupt shopping; research started only when review opens would leave scans unprocessed until the user returns. The deployed companion already combines Federation names with Open Food Facts facts in its barcode lookup, but its interactive `grocy_product_from_barcode` tool requires duplicate and generic-parent decisions. The worker must reuse the read-only lookup logic, not invoke that write tool.

Keep the provider boundary in `grocy-mcp` and the queue, draft, approval, product creation, and commit guard in the Grocy extension. The built-in `OpenFoodFactsBarcodeLookupPlugin` supplies only basic name/image fields and does not include Federation; it is not the sole research path. Add a versioned, authenticated companion research response rather than changing the existing product-form enrichment contract. The response contains bounded candidate names, brand, package information, Open Food Facts categories, source identifiers, and optional safe image evidence. Federation supplies names, not category or nutrition claims. Receipt OCR is supporting evidence for a particular scan, not proof that a provider identified its UPC.

## Queue and draft state

On a new valid unknown scan, insert or reuse a research job keyed by canonical GTIN. Repeated scans continue coalescing the capture line and do not create more jobs. An existing barcode owner bypasses research. Unsupported or checksum-invalid barcodes remain unknown for manual review and never cause automatic provider traffic or product creation. Queue insertion is local and fast; a provider outage never fails the scan.

Persist jobs and drafts in new namespaced, versioned tables under the existing SQLite data path. A job records its canonical code, state (`queued`, `leased`, `ready`, `retryable_failure`, or `needs_input`), bounded attempt count, next retry time, lease expiry, result revision, and safe error code. A draft records validated suggestions and their sources, selected values, related capture line IDs, receipt-text evidence, revision, final product ID when approved or linked, and an append-only actor/time audit. Do not store provider HTML, arbitrary URLs, secrets, or unbounded raw responses. User edits take precedence over later provider retries; approved or linked drafts are immutable as research results.

The companion worker claims a bounded number of jobs through authenticated Grocy endpoints, performs both lookups outside a SQLite write transaction, and submits validated results against the lease and revision. Expired leases return to the queue. Retry temporary failures with bounded backoff; expose **Retry research** for a failed draft. A permanent provider miss becomes a provisional draft rather than an infinite retry. Grocy remains usable when the companion is unavailable. A canceled or committed trip does not receive new review work; shared GTIN research can still serve another active trip.

Receipt OCR may add a proposed name only when a reviewed receipt line is explicitly associated with the scanned capture line or the pairing is unique and unambiguous. OCR corrections update the proposal without overwriting a user's draft edit. If neither provider nor receipt gives a usable name, show the UPC and require a name before approval. Do not infer identity solely from a fuzzy receipt description.

## Review and creation

Add a product-research card beside each unknown selected capture line in Purchase capture review. Show `Researching`, `Ready to review`, `Needs details`, or `Provider unavailable`; the preferred name and alternatives; source labels; suggested brand, package details, image, Grocy Product group, and `grocy_AI` food classification; and possible existing Grocy matches. The user can edit the proposed name and supported fields, choose an existing product, approve a new product, retry, or deselect the scan. Show a final confirmation summary before the create/link write. A provider result never silently chooses an existing product because brands and package sizes can look similar.

Approval requires both `STOCK_PURCHASE` and `MASTER_DATA_EDIT`. In one guarded server operation, recheck the draft revision, current canonical barcode ownership, existing-product choice, product-name uniqueness, active units/location/group, and trip state. If the barcode acquired an owner after research, return that owner for review instead of creating a duplicate. Create a new product through Grocy's established product persistence rules, attach the original scanned barcode while enforcing canonical-GTIN uniqueness, and record the approved product ID and audit event. A failed barcode attachment must not leave a second active product that a retry would recreate. Repeating a successful approval returns the same product ID. Linking an existing product attaches the barcode only after explicit user confirmation and the same ownership check.

Do not guess a generic parent product. Offer existing generic candidates during review, allow the user to choose one when unit-compatible, or leave the new product without a parent for later organization. Do not create a parent merely because a provider category or name resembles one. Product group and `grocy_AI` taxonomy are separate fields: propose a group only when a normalized Open Food Facts category exactly matches one active local group, and propose a taxonomy leaf only through the existing versioned mapping rules. Ambiguous, excluded, or unsupported categories stay unset/Unclassified. The user confirms or changes both before approval. Image and nutrition suggestions retain source attribution and follow the existing bounded media and product-form handling; they must not block product creation when absent.

After approval or linking, the capture line re-resolves to the actual Grocy product. Receipt line allocations still need the user to confirm Include/Ignore, quantity, unit price, and store. A selected unknown line cannot pass server readiness, and a draft alone never satisfies it. Add draft identity/revision and resolved product ownership to the reviewed checksum so a product change after preview invalidates the commit confirmation. Product creation writes no stock or stock log.

## API, security, and compatibility

Add closed, versioned research/draft response shapes through separate capture endpoints; do not add fields to the existing trip and line DTOs, which the browser validates exactly. The worker endpoints use a server-held credential and least-privilege authorization; normal users cannot submit provider results or claim jobs. Approval and linking endpoints use the signed-in user's permissions and write the actor to the module audit. Validate GTINs, field lengths, IDs, source identifiers, provider sizes, and allowed image formats at the Grocy boundary. Keep external requests in the companion and follow the existing private image-handle rules. Never send the Grocy API key, companion key, receipt text, or provider raw body to browser logs.

The current product-form UPC enrichment and manual Create product link remain available. This feature must not alter known-barcode scans, existing products, stock, canceled trips, or committed trips. It introduces no automatic purchase booking and no general catalogue-wide rewrite.

## Verification and rollout

Contract tests cover source precedence, provider miss, malformed or slow responses, bounded retries, lease expiry, restart recovery, duplicate scans, concurrent workers, changed barcode ownership, ambiguous existing matches, manual draft corrections, category mapping, permissions, idempotent approve/link, failed attachment recovery, and no product or stock write before approval. Receipt and commit tests cover OCR name evidence, ignored and deselected lines, multi-receipt trips, approval followed by allocation, stale checksum, and stock remaining unchanged until Commit purchase. Mobile browser tests cover research progress, provisional drafts, corrections, existing-product linking, confirmation, and failure/retry states. Re-run the Grocy PHP contract suite, companion tests, and iPhone-sized capture/review checks.

Release the companion research capability before the Grocy worker and UI. Back up the full persistent `/etc/komodo/grocy` data path and verify a restore rehearsal. Bump the customization version marker when adding routes so the persistent Slim route cache refreshes. Preview a bounded backfill of selected, valid, unknown capture lines in trip #12; then enqueue them without creating products or changing the receipt. Confirm counts and audit records before and after. Verify on a real phone that a provider hit and a provider miss can be reviewed, that an approved barcode re-resolves, and that stock logs do not change before the user's explicit purchase commit.
