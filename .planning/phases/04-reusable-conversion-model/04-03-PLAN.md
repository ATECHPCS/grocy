## Phase Goal

**As a** household user, **I want to** receive a clearly approximate conversion only for my explicitly classified food, **so that** uncertain density estimates never masquerade as exact data.

<objective>
Add sourced food-type profiles and deterministic precedence to the working universal slice while preventing inference from evidence or conflicts.

Purpose: permit narrow, explainable mass↔volume estimates without changing product rows or guessing unknown foods.
Output: profile catalog, taxonomy eligibility guard, precedence/provenance DTO, and graph-resolution tests.
</objective>
