## Phase Goal

**As a** household maintainer, **I want to** export a redacted JSON or CSV snapshot of a bulk plan or preview, **so that** I have independent, human-reviewable recovery evidence even when I skipped a database backup — without that file ever becoming a back-door write path.

<objective>
Deliver the recovery-evidence slice: a redacted, explicitly non-authoritative JSON and CSV snapshot of a plan or preview, served as a permission-checked zero-write read.

Purpose: give the user portable proof of what was reviewed and what changed, while keeping Grocy the sole durable authority.
Output: `ExportPlan` on `GrocyAiBulkService`, a `MASTER_DATA_EDIT`-gated export read on the API controller, and a download control on the review surface.
</objective>
